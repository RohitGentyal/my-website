## Workflow

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Preview: `npx serve . -l 5174`, or the `my-website` entry in `../.claude/launch.json`.
- `index.html` links `style.css?v=…`, `scene.js?v=…` and `loader.js?v=…`. **Bump a file's `?v=` whenever it changes.** Otherwise returning visitors get a stale cached script, and the hero has rendered blank before.
- **Load speed (2026-11-02):** a full scene paint costs ~150–250ms, so it must happen only once on load. The page scrollbar is hidden (I didn't want the grey bar and white strip, and it appearing after the loader narrowed the page and forced a second paint mid-curtain, which froze the page on refresh). `render()` waits for the fonts (1.5s fallback) instead of painting before and again after them. Don't add anything that changes the page width or the hero's size after load. **Memory:** my PC has 7.4 GB RAM (often <1 GB free) and a 2020 AMD integrated-GPU driver; refreshing made Chrome freeze and stop working. So the 3 night layers (`skyNight`, `cloudNight`, `glow`) are only allocated while night shows or is on its way (`paintNight` / `freeNight`); day mode holds 6 full-screen canvases instead of 9. Don't add more full-screen canvases or CSS filters on big layers. **Scroll (2026-11-03):** the bee layer clears only a box around each bee's last spot (`beeMarks`), not the whole canvas every frame, and pointer moves within 150ms of a scroll are ignored (Chrome reports the page sliding under a still cursor as moves, which set off grass and shadow repaints). Any per-frame drawing should repaint only what changed.
- **Sky, clouds, wind, caption (2026-11-03, my asks):**
  - **Sky** (`drawSky(DAY_SKY / NIGHT_SKY)`): deeper teal overhead easing to a hazier, lighter blue at the horizon, and a soft light band just above the hill. Painted once per render. **Rejected:** faint wind-combed wisps/streaks in the sky ("not good").
  - **Clouds** are no longer painted on the sky. Each is its own small canvas pair (day + night, cropped to the cloud) inside `#clouds`, moved only by transform: the tower sways and bobs a few px; small far-off clouds drift right to left at 11·S px/s (7·S on phones; I asked for a bit faster than 8·S) (2 in a high band under the top bar and above my name, 1 low over the horizon, slipping behind the hill; phones get one low one) and wrap around. **No hover effect on clouds** (my call, 2026-11-03: "keep the cloud as it was"). Tried and removed the same day: a nudge away from the cursor, and a hover "vaporize" that tore the cloud apart. Night copies are only painted while night shows. The old full-screen `cloudNight` layer is gone.
  - **Grass sway (WebGL2, `#sway` canvas, "swaying grass" section of scene.js):** all ~24,000 blades (slope blades and tufts) are uploaded to the graphics card once per render, and a shader bends each one every frame, so the whole field moves at ~0ms of main-thread script. Modelled on my reference (a breeze through barley): a slow swell rolls right to left (wavelength 520·S px, 6s) over a longer one (1400·S px, 14s), the field leans left 0.09–0.61 rad and rocks (tips swing ~10–18px; I asked for "a bit more" twice, after 0.06–0.36 and 0.08–0.5, and 0.03–0.13 read as not moving), each blade has a flutter of ±0.07 rad, and crest blades are up to 9% lighter. **The animation loop (`buzz`) is started at the end of `render()`:** the scene paints after the fonts load, which can be after the hero's on-screen check, and before this fix the bees, cloud drift and grass sway all sat frozen on some loads. **Cursor:** every blade near the cursor keeps its own spring, as in the original 2D version (`brush`/`step`); the springing blades' bends go to the shader as one per-blade float array (`bends`, uploaded only while something moves). **Rejected:** one shared cursor "push" in the shader ("not as smooth as it was": the bent patch jumped with the mouse and blades snapped back). While brushing, the shadow layer repaints at most every 80ms plus once when the grass settles (a full repaint every frame cost ~7ms), and the 2D repaint gathers only flowers and leaves. Flowers and leaves stay on the 2D `#touch` layer and react as before; they now always sit above the blades. Night tint is the same CSS filter as the hill. If WebGL2 is missing or the context is lost, the blades are painted on `#touch`, still, as before. **Rejected:** CSS light bands sweeping over the grass ("too heavy"), and 2D-canvas per-blade wind (43–62ms a frame).
  - **Corner caption:** cream with a soft shadow on the grass; as the hill fades into the page on scroll, it blends to the page ink (`--over-page`, set in `parallax`). **Rejected:** dark ink text on the grass ("looks really bad").
  - Checked at 1920×960: a steady 60fps with no long frames and no script time in them, with the bees, clouds and grass sway all running.
- **Night switch and dark mode speed (2026-11-15, my note: switching to dark lagged):** profiled in real Chrome on my AMD Radeon iGPU (CDP traces, frame times, long-animation-frame entries; scripts in the session scratchpad, not the repo). Look unchanged: day, night and mid-dusk screenshots match the old ones to rounding.
  - **Causes:** (1) the shadows were re-stroked every frame the light moved (~6,000 round-capped lines on a 2D canvas: 50–60ms of GPU a frame); (2) the moonlight's crest sheen blurred each of its ~300 strokes separately, ~1s of GPU on the first frame of every switch to night; (3) the night tint was a CSS filter changing every frame on four full-screen layers; (4) the night sky and clouds were painted all at once on the click.
  - **Fixes:** (1) the shadows are drawn by the graphics card with the swaying grass (`shadeGL`/`bakeShade` in scene.js): the same shapes with exact anti-aliased coverage, each batch merged like one fill, the same colours and order, then the same Gaussian blur as the old CSS `blur(1.4·S px)`, drawn under the blades. `#shade` is hidden and freed then; it is still the fallback without WebGL2 (`drawShade`'s 2D branch). They now follow brushed grass every frame. (2) the sheen strokes go on a scratch strip that is blurred once. (3) hill, shadows, grass and flowers sit in one `.land` wrapper that carries the one tint filter. (4) `paintNight(true)` paints one step a frame as the switch starts (still near-invisible then), and hovering or focusing the moon button paints them ahead, freed again 1.5s after the cursor leaves without switching. Also `cast()` reuses one object (it runs ~9,000 times a frame).
  - **Numbers, 1904×865:** to night 6–15fps (frames up to 0.7–1.6s) → 54–59fps; to day 22–28fps → 58–60fps; scroll in dark 19–59fps (stalls after a switch) → 60fps; brushing the grass at night 32 → 54fps. With a 4× slower CPU the switch holds 41–54fps. The odd 100–200ms frame left has no script in it and shows up on an idle page too (this PC's background load). Not checked on a phone or another GPU.
- Check at desktop and 375px widths, then commit and push in the same turn. The live site updates about a minute after a push; refresh once if it looks stale.
- `gh` CLI isn't signed in; `git push` works.

- **One sunset for the whole page + switch-then-scroll lag (2026-11-19, my asks: "if the sun is going down, the entire page's sun is going down"; the caption changed the instant I clicked; lag when I switch and scroll at once):**
  - **Causes found (CDP, long-animation-frame attribution):** the footer (meadow.js) repainted everything on every theme switch, 1.7s on the main thread; the hero's night timeline only ran while the hero was on screen; the page colours ran on their own CSS timer (1.8s +0.4s) and only body colour animated, so most text snapped.
  - **Now:** the switch runs on its own frame loop in scene.js (`nightLoop`), on screen or not; `applyNight` also drives a fixed warm `.wash` over the page below the hero (peak 22%), the footer (`--nt` on .foot fades its night sky and night clouds; its land gets the hill's exact filter string) and the corner caption (`setLabel`: turns to "Night" halfway, with a 0.26s fade). Page colour tokens are registered with @property and transition on :root.themed (added on click only, so no animation on load): surfaces on the full 2.8s curve, words held until halfway then turned in 0.5s so they never sink into the background. `.live::before` fades instead of display:none.
  - **Footer** paints once for both themes (day sky, night sky with stars on #meadowSkyNight, every cloud as a day/night pair in a .cl div), in idle steps, when within 900px; the theme never repaints it. Its WebGL grass canvas is sized BEFORE getContext (resizing a WebGL canvas after its context exists cost ~2s on my AMD iGPU); a later size change swaps in a fresh canvas. Path test uses a 2px mask instead of isPointInPath per blade. Hero initGL also sizes first.
  - **Loading:** only the first screenshot of each Work card loads with the page; the rest (data-src) load when the card is on screen (work.js). Genzo shots are WebP (125KB, was 288KB PNG). First load 586KB -> ~220KB.
  - **Measured (headless Chrome on the real GPU):** switch then scroll: worst frame 1.2-1.5s -> ~0.1-0.35s (switch itself); scroll top -> footer vs live: p95 33ms -> 17ms, slow frames 16-17 -> 11-12.
